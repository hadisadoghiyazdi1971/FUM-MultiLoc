# Security and Privacy Notice

The future FUM-MultiLoc dataset may contain information that requires review before public distribution, especially visual observations, indoor maps, Wi-Fi identifiers, cellular/BTS measurements, and semantic room information.

No unreviewed raw research data should be committed to this repository.

Potential public-release transformations include:

- replacing personal room labels with anonymous room IDs and functional categories;
- removing or pseudonymizing network identifiers;
- minimizing cellular infrastructure identifiers to the fields scientifically required by the benchmark;
- blurring or removing faces, nameplates, screens, documents, QR codes, and other identifying visual regions;
- publishing sanitized research floor maps instead of operational/internal maps.

A formal privacy and release review must be completed before `v1.0.0`.
